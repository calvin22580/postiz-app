Kanosena design language · v1 · 20/11/2026

Story Mode.

Kanosena is built from the tools our customers already love: highlighted story text, stickers, scribbles and chunky type on electric violet. The fun lives in Kanosena's own screens. Every decision the owner makes is in plain words. Every customer post stays entirely their own.

01

Five rules that make it Kanosena

These came straight out of six rounds of testing with four customer personas. When a choice isn't covered elsewhere on this page, these decide it.

Our brand frames the work. Theirs fills it.

Stickers, highlights and scribbles never touch a customer's post. Their colours, fonts and photos always win.

Fun in the frame, calm at the decision.

Celebrate around the edges. At the moment of choosing, there's one clear button and plain words.

Say when it goes out.

Every approved post shows the day and time, written out: "Goes out Mon 23/11 at 07:30".

Stickers mean something.

A sticker is a status (waiting, approved, new, posted), never decoration. One per post at most.

Warm, quick, a bit cheeky. Never vague.

A creative friend who's great at social. British English, honest about what we did and what we need.

The sticker K

The K is a sticker: butter yellow with an ink outline, tilted six degrees, with a small pink star. It peels, tilts and slaps onto Kanosena's own screens. The wordmark is set in Dela Gothic One.

03

Electric violet, butter, and a little pink

Violet is Kanosena. Butter is for highlights and celebration. Pink and pool are small accents for stickers and scribbles. Most screens are cloud and white, so the customer's own colours stand out.

Tokens

One file that the homepage, the app and the emails all read from. In the app these sit over upstream's colours through our brand style file, so no upstream component has to change.
