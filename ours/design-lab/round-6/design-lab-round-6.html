Story Mode, grown up.

Story Mode is the favourite. In round 3 the testers marked it down because its stickers spilled onto customer posts and the app was harder to scan. Rounds 4 and 5 fixed exactly those problems for the other designs. Story Mode 2 gets the same fixes, and is shown here next to First Class 4, the testers' winner.

What the testers taught us

Our brand frames the work. The customer's brand fills it.

Every design gained around two points once its motifs stayed off customer posts. Graham needs to know at a glance what to press, and that a post is still to come. Everyone wanted Edit next to Approve.

What Story Mode 2 changes

Stickers for Kanosena. Plain words for decisions.

Stickers, highlights and scribbles now live only on Kanosena's own screens. The week's progress fills like story segments. Approve is violet, never red, with Edit beside it, and every approved post says "Goes out Mon 23/11 at 07:30".
