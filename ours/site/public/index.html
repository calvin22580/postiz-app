How it works

Four steps. The only one that needs you each week is the third.

  1. We read your website

    Give us your web address. We build a short profile of your business, and every fact shows where it came from. You can edit any of it.

  2. We write your week

    A week of posts and simple branded graphics, in your colours, written only from your profile. UK dates and seasons are taken into account.

  3. You approve

    Approve, edit, reject or ask for a new version of each post. Only the posts you approve are scheduled.

  4. We publish and report back

    Approved posts go out at the planned time to the accounts you connected, and we show you how they did.

You stay in charge

It's your business and your name. We built Kanosena around that.

  • Nothing goes out without your yes

    Every post waits for your approval. You can change or cancel a scheduled post any time before it goes out.

  • No made-up facts

    Posts are written from what your own website says. We don't invent prices, offers, reviews or claims about your business.

  • Your accounts stay yours

    You connect your accounts through each platform's own login screen, and you can disconnect them at any time.

  • Your data isn't for sale

    We never send messages on your behalf, and never use your platform data for advertising or to train AI models.

Questions

Do I need to write anything?

No. You give us your web address and we write the posts. You read them, change anything you like, and approve the ones you're happy with.

What if my website is out of date?

You can edit your business profile at any time. Posts are written from the profile, so fixing a fact there fixes it for every post after that.

Which accounts can I connect?

Facebook Pages, Instagram business or creator accounts linked to a Facebook Page, and LinkedIn Pages. Personal profiles aren't supported.

Can I stop at any time?

Yes. You can disconnect your accounts whenever you like, and ask us to delete your data. See how to delete your data.
